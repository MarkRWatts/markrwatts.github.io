Philosophy

Security that earns trust rather than demands it

I believe security functions succeed when they're built on trust rather than authority. Teams that operate as gatekeepers eventually get circumvented, while those that help the business move faster and more confidently become indispensable.

I focus on early engagement with project teams rather than late-stage compliance reviews, and measure a function's value through business outcomes rather than activity metrics alone. I'm equally committed to developing the next generation of security leaders through inclusive hiring and deliberate team development.
